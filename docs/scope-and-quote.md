## Timeline

About 4 weeks from the day we receive your brand assets and product list; the clock starts then, not at deposit.

1. **Week 1 — Design.** Kick-off call, your brand applied, and a clickable homepage and product-page preview for sign-off.
2. **Week 2 — Build.** Theme built on a private development store; you get a preview link to click around.
3. **Week 3 — Set-up.** Products, photos, collections and care guides loaded; payments, courier and VAT connected; your first round of feedback applied.
4. **Week 4 — Launch.** Test orders to several cities, mobile checks, final feedback, a training session, then the store goes live on your domain.
