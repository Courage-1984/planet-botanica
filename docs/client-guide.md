# Running your Planet Botanica shop

A plain-language guide to the day-to-day jobs. Everything happens in your Shopify admin; nothing here needs code.

---

## 1. Adding or editing a plant

**Products → Add product** (or open an existing one).

1. **Title** — start with the Latin name, e.g. *Dionaea muscipula 'B52'*. Add the common name in brackets if you like.
2. **Photos** — at least two. The first shows on cards; the second appears when a shopper hovers. Use photos you own (or have a licence for).
3. **Product type** — the genus, e.g. `Dionaea`. This decides which genus collection the plant lands in.
4. **Price, stock and sizes** — add a *Pot size* option for seedling / established / specimen sizes.
5. **Tags** — `Coll: Beginners` puts it in *Beginner-friendly*, `Coll: Rare Species` in *Rare species*, `Coll: Tropicals` in *Tropicals*.
6. **Care data** — scroll to **Metafields** at the bottom and fill in what you know. Each one appears on the product page:

| Field | Where it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Latin name | Under the title (when the title doesn't already include it). **Also marks the product as a live plant**, which switches on the dispatch countdown. |
| Difficulty (1–5) | The three-dot meter: 1–2 Easy, 3 Intermediate, 4–5 Expert. Also used by filters and the "Find your carnivore" quiz. |
| Trap type | Cards, product page, filters and quiz. |
| Temperature group | "Climate" on the care sheet, filters and quiz (highland, lowland, temperate…). |
| Light, Water, Substrate, Humidity, Temperature range | The care sheet. |
| Dormancy required | The care sheet, plus a "dormancy season" notice on the product page in winter. |
| CITES listed | A CITES badge on the product page. |
| Origin, Altitude, Growth rate, Clone ID | The "specimen" part of the care sheet. Origin is also the caption on product cards. |
| Care guide | Long-form notes that open under "Read the full care guide". |

Supplies (soil, pots) simply leave the plant fields empty — they won't show a care sheet or dispatch countdown.

## 2. Writing a News post or a care guide

**Content → Blog posts → Create blog post.**

- **Blog** — choose **News** for nursery news, restocks, events and stories, or **Care guides** for how-to articles.
- **Title and content** — keep the first paragraph short: it's used as the excerpt on cards.
- **Featured image** — shows on the homepage, the blog page and at the top of the post. Landscape works best.
- **Tags** — e.g. *Restock*, *Events*, *Dionaea*. Readers can filter posts by tag.
- **Plants in this post** — at the bottom of the editor, under *Metafields*, pick the plants you mention. They appear as a shoppable row under the post.
- **Visibility** — save as *Hidden* to keep a draft; set *Visible* to publish.

There's a draft post in **News** called *"Welcome to the Planet Botanica news"* — edit it into your first real post, or delete it.

The homepage **News** section appears automatically once the first News post is published.

## 3. Menus

**Content → Menus.** The shop uses its own menus:

- **Planet Botanica — main menu** — the top navigation. Items with a second and third level (like *Shop plants → By genus → Venus flytraps*) open as a large "mega menu"; items with just a second level (like *Learn*) open as a small dropdown.
- **Planet Botanica — footer** and **Customer care** — the two link columns in the footer.

Drag to reorder, and nest an item under another to turn it into a dropdown entry.

The picture and text in the mega menu ("New to carnivores? Start here") are set in **Online Store → Themes → Customize → Header → Mega menu promo**.

## 4. Shipping live plants

**Online Store → Themes → Customize → ⚙ Theme settings → Live plant shipping.**

- **Dispatch days** and **cut-off hour** drive the "Order within 2h 14m for dispatch today" message on plant pages and in the cart.
- **Pause live plant dispatch** — switch on for heatwaves, long weekends or the December break. Orders stay open; shoppers see your pause message instead of the countdown. **Remember to switch it off again.**
- **Dormancy season** months and message.
- **Cart → Free shipping threshold** — keep this in step with your real shipping rates.

## 5. Colours, fonts, logo and wording

**Online Store → Themes → Customize.**

- **⚙ Theme settings** — logo, colours, fonts, button shapes, business details (shown in the footer), social links and the WhatsApp chat button.
- Click any section on the page to edit its text and images, drag sections to reorder, or **Add section** for new ones (FAQ, testimonials, image with text…).
- Only publish real customer reviews in the *Testimonials* section.

## 6. Filters and the "Find your carnivore" quiz

Filters live in the free **Search & Discovery** app (**Apps → Search & Discovery → Filters**). The quiz on the homepage and collection pages sends shoppers to filtered results, so its answers must match your filter values:

- *Where will your plant grow?* → Temperature group
- *How green are your thumbs?* → Difficulty (1 = Easy, 3 = Intermediate, 5 = Expert)
- *Which trap fascinates you most?* → Trap type

Edit the questions in **Customize → the quiz section**. If you add a new temperature group or trap type, add it to a quiz answer too.

In Search & Discovery you can also choose **Complementary products** for each plant (soil, pots, kits) — they appear in the "Complete the setup" row on the product page.
